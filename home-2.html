HOW GRANDMOTHER'S RECIPES ARE PRESERVED

Industrial factories cut corners with synthetic acetic acid, artificial red colors, and high-speed blenders that scorch natural spice oils. Here is why Rivaya remains unapologetically handcrafted:

1

Cold-Pressed Sesame Oil Seal

Unrefined wooden-mill gingelly oil contains sesamol, a natural antioxidant that preserves raw mangoes without chemicals.

2

Stone Grinding Preserves Aromatics

Mustard and roasted fenugreek are crushed slowly on granite stones, avoiding temperature rise to keep essential oils potent.

3

Solar Curing on Rooftop Terraces

Jars sit in warm sunlight every morning. The gentle thermal cycle activates natural fermentation that commercial factories cannot replicate.
