Preserving Freshness

PRODUCT CARE AND STORAGE

Because our pickles and papads contain no artificial chemical stabilizers, customers should follow the specific storage instructions supplied with each package.

Storage Conditions

Keep pickle jars in a cool, dark, and dry pantry. Avoid direct sunlight or humid stovetops. Refrigeration is generally not required if the oil seal is maintained.

Handling & Spoon Etiquette

Always use a thoroughly clean, dry wooden or stainless steel spoon. Never introduce moisture or wet cutlery into the jar, as natural preparations rely on zero water contact.

Papad Crispness Care

Store sun-dried papads and appalams in airtight metal or glass tins away from damp air. If slight humidity is absorbed, sun-warm them for 10 minutes before roasting or frying.
